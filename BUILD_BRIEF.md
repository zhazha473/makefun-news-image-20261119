- NOT a newspaper column layout (the winners' format). Ours is an ATLAS + TIMELINE:
  a real world map drawn from country outlines as the main visual, the 14 biggest
  stories pinned by number to where they happened, and the remaining 22 stories in
  three columns under "THE DAY SWEEPS WEST" — Asia-Pacific, then Europe/Africa/
  Middle East, then the Americas — the order Thursday crossed the world.
- 36 stories total (the top-50 median), across 6 continents plus deep space
  (Voyager 1). The count is stated in the masthead and the title.
- The title counts stories and names the structure in our own words
  ("atlas and a 24-hour timeline"), not the winners' "front page and a map".
- No winner's title, image, code, text or layout is copied. Every headline is
  written fresh from reporting first published 19 November 2026.
